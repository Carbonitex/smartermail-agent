Your OpenRouter key

Get one at openrouter.ai/keys. It stays in this browser tab (sessionStorage) and is sent straight to OpenRouter — this site's server never sees it and never pays for your tokens. Closing the tab forgets it.

Sessions are held in the server's memory only and expire after 30 minutes idle (12 hours maximum). The conversation itself lives in this tab and is gone when you close it.
